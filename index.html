The problem

Your work arrives from too many directions at once.

Emails, meetings, phone calls, people stopping by your desk. Each one creates an action — but there's no single place that holds all of it together.

01

Actions get buried in email threads

A request sent on Tuesday is invisible by Thursday, scrolled past and forgotten in a chain of twenty replies.

02

Nothing tells you what to do first

Without a way to separate high-impact actions from low ones, the loudest email wins — not the most important one.

03

Follow-ups fall through the cracks

You chase a supplier, they don't reply. Three days later you've forgotten you were waiting — and the job is at risk.

04

You become the system

Your brain ends up holding everything together — what's outstanding, what's waiting, what's overdue. That's not sustainable.

How it works

Four steps. No setup required.

Priorit works on top of how you already operate. You don't change your inbox or your systems — you just stop having to hold everything in your head.

1

Tell Priorit what's important right now

A machine that's down, a supplier that's unreliable, a job that can't be late. Add a note and Priorit factors it in every time it reads an email.

2

Paste an email or request

One message or a full thread. Priorit reads it and pulls out the specific actions that belong to you.

3

Review your tasks and draft replies

Each task comes with a priority level, a due date if one was mentioned, and a draft reply ready to check and send.

4

Track, follow up and stay on top

Mark tasks as waiting, set follow-up reminders, add notes — and see at a glance what's overdue, in progress and done.

What's included

Built around how operational work actually happens.

Email task extraction
Paste any email or thread. Priorit pulls out the actions that belong to you, written as clear first-person tasks.
Draft replies
Every task comes with a suggested reply in plain language, addressed to the right person. Check it, adjust if needed, send.
Chain summaries
A plain English summary of a full email thread so you understand the background without re-reading twenty messages.
Context Board
Persistent notes on known issues and priorities that Priorit factors into every task it generates.
Waiting status
Mark a task as waiting, note who it's on, and set a date to follow up. Priorit flags it when it's due.
Task aging
See how long each task has been open. Tasks sitting untouched for five or more days are highlighted automatically.
Snapshot dashboard
Active tasks, high priority items, overdue tasks and late purchase orders — visible the moment you open the app.
